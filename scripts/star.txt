|These '''3''' Special Heads are not obtainable by completing Furbottom Features but instead were only available during the Steam Summer Sale 2014 (19th-30th of June) and could potentially be gained as an item drop from crafting a SSS'14 badge. 
The Lava Lamp was an ultra-rare 'precious' drop so the chances of getting one were...microscopic. Only about 600 of Lava Lamps were 'crafted' during the sale and that is the finite amount of them. The prisoner will '''not''' be re-released.

Scuba Steve as of 'common' rarity so there was a high probability of obtaining one. About 200k of Scuba Steves were 'crafted' during the sale and that is the finite amount of them. The prisoner will '''not''' be re-released.

The only current way of unlocking these 3 prisoners is by purchasing them on the Steam Market place.
